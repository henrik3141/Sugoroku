壽出世双六
Kotobuki Shusse Sugoroku · “Longevity & Success” — a Tokugawa life-course game

In the Tokugawa period, a 双六 (sugoroku) print was not a race around a track. It was a map of a life. You are born into a station, you roll, and the board tells you where a person of that station could plausibly go next — up through headship, trade and scholarship, or down through the pleasure quarters, bankruptcy and the begging bowl.

Each player takes one of the six characters printed in the Start square and begins in that character's own cell. On your turn you roll a die and read your current cell's instructions: only certain numbers lead anywhere. Roll a number that isn't listed and nothing happens — roll again.

Three things can end a life course:
長者 (Finish) — a wealthy house, you win  ·  Cell 5 隠居 — honorable retirement  ·  Cell 45 願人坊主 — the mendicant monk, game over.

Board: 壽出世双六, designed by Utagawa Kuniteru （國輝画）, published by Eikyūdō （榮久堂版）. All cell artwork on this page is detail from that print.
